About

Research papers are written for other researchers.

That is the whole problem. A study can be genuinely interesting and still be locked behind jargon, hedged phrasing, and a structure built for people who already know the field. The findings are not the hard part. The writing is.

So that is what we do. A member picks a recently published paper, reads it closely enough to know what it actually claims, and writes a version a high schooler can follow. Without turning it into a headline that promises more than the study found.

We keep the limitations in. If a result came from forty people, or mice, or a single lab, we say so. Translating research badly is worse than not translating it at all, and most science coverage aimed at young people gets this wrong.
